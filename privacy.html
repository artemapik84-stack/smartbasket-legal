SmartBasket Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-15

What we store

When you use online features, SmartBasket stores your authentication email, display name or avatar you choose, shopping lists, family membership and related app data in Supabase. In local mode, lists are kept on your device.

How we use data

We use this data only to provide sign in, synchronization, family features and the shopping and recipe tools you request. We do not sell personal data. The current app does not add advertising or third-party analytics.

Sharing and security

Supabase processes online data on behalf of the app. Use the app only with lists and information you are comfortable sharing with your family group. Keep your account credentials private.

Deletion

You can remove local data from the app settings. If you use an online account, use the in-app account deletion or contact the project owner through the public repository.

Changes

This policy may be updated when SmartBasket adds online services. The date above shows the latest revision.
